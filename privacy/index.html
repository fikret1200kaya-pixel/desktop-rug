Desktop Rug — Privacy Policy / Gizlilik Politikası

Last updated / Son güncelleme: October 7, 2026

English

Desktop Rug is published by EfkaAjans.

We do not collect, store, transmit or share any personal data.

  • The app does not connect to the internet and has no accounts, analytics, advertising or tracking.
  • The app does not read, move, change or delete your files. The rug is drawn on top of your desktop; icons underneath are only covered visually.
  • To remember your preferences, the app saves a few settings on your own computer only: the selected rug model, the rug's position, size and folded shape, and whether it should start with Windows. These never leave your device and are removed when you uninstall the app.
  • The app reads your mouse cursor position only while it is running, only to know whether the pointer is over the rug. This is not recorded or sent anywhere.

Microsoft Store handles purchases, trials and downloads under Microsoft's own privacy statement: https://privacy.microsoft.com

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this address.
